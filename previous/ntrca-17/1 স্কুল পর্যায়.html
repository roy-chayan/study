৬৫Kalam is as strong as Salam. (Make it Comparative)

  1. কSalam is not stronger than Kalam.
  2. খSalam is stronger than Kalam.
  3. গKalam is not stronger than Salam.
  4. ঘKalam is stronger than Salam.

উত্তরক) Salam is not stronger than Kalam.

ব্যাখ্যা

  • as … as (সমান) বাক্যকে comparative করতে কর্তা দুটির জায়গা বদলে not + comparative বসে।
  • Kalam is as strong as Salam → Salam is not stronger than Kalam.

৬৮Over-flooding is one of the worst problems in our country. (Positive)

  1. কOver-flooding is worse than any other problem.
  2. খNo other problem in our country is as bad as over-flooding.
  3. গVery few problems in our country are as bad as over-flooding.
  4. ঘOver-flooding is a very worse problem in our country.

উত্তরগ) Very few problems in our country are as bad as over-flooding.

ব্যাখ্যা

  • one of the + superlative (worst) → positive: Very few + plural noun + are as + positive (bad) + as।
  • খ মানে ‘সবচেয়ে খারাপ’ — অর্থ বদলে যায়; ঘ-তে ‘very worse’ ভুল।

৬৯What can not be cured must be endured. (Make it Active)

  3. গWe must endure what we cannot cure.
  4. ঘWe must be endured what we cannot cure.

উত্তরগ) We must endure what we cannot cure.

ব্যাখ্যা

  • অর্থ: যার প্রতিকার নেই, তা সহ্য করতেই হবে। Passive-এ কর্তা নেই, তাই active-এ we বসে।
  • must be endured → must endure; cannot be cured → cannot cure: We must endure what we cannot cure.
